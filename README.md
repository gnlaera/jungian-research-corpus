# Jungian Research Corpus

A structured research corpus on Jung and later Jungian interpreters.

This repository publishes a five-volume portable edition derived from ten authoritative JSRL canonical Markdown masters. The ten canonical masters were combined without rewriting their scholarly content. Each authoritative master is embedded byte-for-byte inside one of the five publication volumes and remains recoverable exactly.

## Published volumes

1. `JUNG_00_CONTROL_ROUTER_AND_TERMS.md` - evidence hierarchy, terminology, routing, and deployment guidance.
2. `JUNG_01_JUNG_CORE_THEORY_AND_PRACTICE.md` - Jungian psychic architecture, dynamics, dreams, psychotherapy, transference, type, and inferior function.
3. `JUNG_02_POSTJUNGIAN_RELATIONSHIP_AND_DEVELOPMENT.md` - relationship, projection, dialogue, couples work, and post-Jungian developmental perspectives.
4. `JUNG_03_ATTACHMENT_EMOTION_AND_COUPLE_THERAPY.md` - adult attachment, Johnson EFCT, Greenberg Emotion-Focused Therapy, complex activation, and emotion regulation.
5. `JUNG_04_APPLIED_SELF_RELATIONSHIP_AND_REPAIR.md` - applied self-analysis, individuation, projection, couple cycles, rupture, and repair.

## Portable research use

The five Markdown volumes can also be used as a compact research corpus in notebook and retrieval systems. `JUNG_00_CONTROL_ROUTER_AND_TERMS.md` supplies evidence hierarchy and routing rules; the remaining volumes supply theory, post-Jungian development, contemporary attachment/emotion/couple frameworks, and applied synthesis.

The GitHub packaging and five-volume consolidation did not rewrite, summarize, shorten, modernize, correct, or otherwise alter the embedded scholarly contents of the ten canonical masters. See `GITHUB_PAGES_QA.md` for reconstruction and safety tests.
